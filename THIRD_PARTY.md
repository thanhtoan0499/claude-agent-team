# Third-party material

Some role skills in `skills/` are condensed and adapted from the open-source projects below. Each adapted skill carries a one-line
`Adapted from ...` comment under its frontmatter. Changes: shortened, tool and process references removed (agents here have no Skill tool,
no web and no way to ask the user), human-partner steps replaced by `NEEDS_DECISION`, team report contract added. The text is
rewritten, not copied verbatim.
